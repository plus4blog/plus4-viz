Skill-driven Corollary Course Metrics

Rather than treating courses as static, interchangeable entities, this model identifies corollary courses at the individual skill level. We find those courses that share playing characteristics with our target course and use a player's performance history at those analogues to infer likely performance at the target. The "skill-driven" layer means the correlation isn't just course-to-course similarity — it's filtered through which skills each course rewards.

Some players love Bermuda grass. Some love hitting the ball left-to-right. Some are better than others from the deep rough, and some excel from tight greenside lies. Each course rewards these traits independently — now, we capture and score them individually.
